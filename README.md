### 🔬 Research

#### Molecular Dynamics of the Lennard-Jones Fluid from Scratch
*The Jackson Group, UIUC · Chemistry research*

As part of my chemistry research at UIUC, I completed a research rotation with [The Jackson Group](https://thejacksonlab.github.io/), where I wrote a 3D molecular dynamics simulation of liquid argon from scratch in Python. The project was reviewed and approved by the group.  
This includes:
- A Lennard-Jones force field with a shifted force cutoff and periodic boundary conditions
- A velocity Verlet integrator, with an energy drift study to choose the time step
- Equilibration with a velocity rescaling thermostat, then production runs in the NVE ensemble
- Computing the radial distribution function g(r) and reproducing the liquid structure from Rahman's 1964 study of liquid argon

Project spec: [Rotation Project (PDF)](https://thejacksonlab.github.io/assets/RotationProject-1.pdf)

#### Machine Learning + Molecular Dynamics
*High school research project*

I explored how neural networks can approximate potential energy surfaces with physics-level accuracy.  
This includes:
- Running molecular dynamics simulations to generate position-energy datasets
- Implementing radial and angular symmetry functions
- Training Behler–Parrinello style neural networks in PyTorch

This project helped me combine my interests in computing, physics, and chemistry while learning how AI can accelerate scientific discovery.
